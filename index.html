A Note From the Founder

A note from the founder

“Your Vision, Crafted.”

That line closes our 2026 catalogue, and it is the whole idea behind Gamosa. Every piece here starts as somebody else's idea — a company logo, a family name, a graduation year, a storefront sign — and our work is to cut, engrave and print it into something worth keeping.

We work across materials rather than specialising in one: metal, acrylic, MDF, thermosteel, glass, granite and silk. That range is deliberate. It lets one order cover a keychain, a trophy, a reception sign and a gift combo, all finished to the same standard.

Ujjal Moni Bordoloi
Founder, Gamosa
